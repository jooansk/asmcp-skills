# Conveyor station: questions for the user

- How many part sensors, and where (infeed, stop position, end of belt)?
- Is there a stopper, or does the belt stop on the sensor alone?
- Belt motor: on/off, two speeds, or a servo axis?
- How long without a part counts as "belt empty" during homing?
- What happens to a part that is on the belt at a production stop: stays, or runs to the stop position?
- Which signal tells the conveyor that the next station took the part?
